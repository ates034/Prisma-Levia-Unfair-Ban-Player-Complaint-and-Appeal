Prisma Levia Unfair Ban Complaint

This page documents a player's complaint regarding an allegedly unjustified ban on the Prisma Levia server. The player is requesting a fair review of the suspension and a clear explanation of the decision.

Concerns About the Account Ban

The player claims that their account was banned for allegedly using a macro without being provided with sufficient evidence to understand or verify the accusation. The suspension has raised concerns about transparency and the fairness of the enforcement process.

Request for a Fair Review

Players facing account suspensions should have a reasonable opportunity to understand the allegations and request a review. This complaint calls for a clear explanation, a transparent assessment of the available evidence, and reconsideration if the ban was issued in error.

Prisma Levia Player Ban Appeal

This page provides a place to document concerns about the Prisma Levia ban and raise awareness of the importance of fair moderation, reliable anti-cheat decisions, and transparent player support.

Conclusion

The purpose of this complaint is to seek clarification and a fair review of the disputed ban. The allegations described here represent the player's account of events and have not been independently verified.
